Nexar.io Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026

Nexar.io is a math-learning app. You do not need to create a Nexar.io account to use it.

Learning data on your device

The app stores your chosen display name, selected course, lesson completion, XP, gems, goals, streaks, and learning activity locally on your device. Nexar.io does not send this learning data to a developer-operated server and does not provide cloud progress synchronization.

You can change your display name in Progress. Deleting the app removes its locally stored learning data. Any device backups you choose to make are managed by your device and backup provider.

Purchases

Optional course purchases and Lifetime Access use Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple processes payment and provides the app with transaction information needed to check your access, restore purchases, and respond to refunds or revocations. Nexar.io does not receive your payment-card details. Purchase ownership is managed through your Apple Account; deleting local learning data does not cancel a purchase.

Apple's handling of purchase and account information is covered by Apple's own privacy policies: Apple Privacy Policy.

Advertising, analytics, and tracking

The app does not include advertisements, analytics SDKs, cross-app tracking, social profiles, or a developer-operated account service. It does not send learning activity to the developer. Apple may provide diagnostics or feedback through its own services according to your settings and choices.

External links and support

If you open an external website, that website's privacy policy applies. If you choose to contact support or send feedback, the information you include is used to respond to your request. Your email provider and our email provider process that correspondence. Contact us if you want to request deletion of support correspondence; some information may need to be retained to resolve a dispute or meet legal obligations. Please do not send passwords, payment-card information, or other information that is unnecessary for support.

This website

This website does not use analytics scripts, advertising, contact forms, or browser storage. When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub Pages information and the GitHub Privacy Statement. This hosting activity is separate from the app’s on-device learning data.

Changes

This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change. Its effective date appears above.
