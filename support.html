Support & contact

A little help along the way.

Contact us

Email preston@radonresearchkorp.com for app support, lesson corrections, or purchase questions.

Learn math with worked examples and guided practice in Algebra, Precalculus, Calculus I–III, and Linear Algebra.

Restore a course or Lifetime Access

Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase. Connect to the internet and tap Restore Purchases in Store, a purchase screen, or Progress. Lifetime Access includes all current and future courses. Individual course purchases do not reduce the Lifetime price.

A purchase is unavailable

Check your internet connection and try Check availability again. If the problem continues, open Help with this purchase and include the displayed support code when contacting support. Do not send Apple Account passwords or payment information.

Progress and profiles

Learning progress stays on your device and does not sync across devices. Updating the app normally preserves it. Deleting the app removes local progress, even though eligible purchases can be restored. You can edit your name in Progress.

Wide equations

Long equations can be scrolled horizontally so that the full expression remains available. Text follows the device's text-size settings.

Report a lesson issue

Include the course, lesson title, problem and step, expected answer, and a screenshot if useful. Do not include private information in screenshots. For an app problem, include the app version, iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, and the steps that led to the issue.
